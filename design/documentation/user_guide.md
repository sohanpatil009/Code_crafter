## 📷 Taking Photos for Best Results

### 📋 Photo Guidelines

#### ✅ Good Photos
```
🍃 Focus on diseased leaf
📏 Fill the frame with leaf
☀️ Use good lighting (natural light best)
📱 Hold phone steady
🎯 Center the leaf in viewfinder
🔍 Show disease symptoms clearly
```

#### ❌ Avoid These
```
🌫️ Blurry or shaky photos
🌑 Too dark or too bright
📐 Leaf too small in frame
🌿 Multiple leaves overlapping
💧 Water drops on lens
🤏 Too close or too far
```

## ❓ Help & Support

### 🆘 Common Issues & Solutions

#### Problem: Blurry Photos
```
Solution:
1. Clean camera lens
2. Hold phone steady
3. Use good lighting
4. Tap to focus before capturing
5. Move closer to leaf
```

#### Problem: Wrong Disease Detection
```
Solution:
1. Retake photo with better lighting
2. Focus on diseased area only
3. Remove water drops from leaf
4. Try different angle
5. Ensure leaf fills frame
```

#### Problem: Audio Not Playing
```
Solution:
1. Check volume settings
2. Ensure internet connection
3. Try different language
4. Restart the app
5. Check phone's audio settings
```

#### Problem: App in Wrong Language
```
Solution:
1. Go to Settings ⚙️
2. Tap Language & Region 🌐
3. Select correct language
4. App will restart automatically
```

## 🎯 Tips for Best Results

### 📸 Photography Tips

#### Lighting
```
✅ Best: Natural daylight (not direct sun)
✅ Good: Bright indoor light
❌ Avoid: Dark shadows, harsh sunlight
❌ Avoid: Artificial yellow light
```

#### Positioning
```
✅ Hold phone 6-12 inches from leaf
✅ Keep leaf flat and visible
✅ Fill frame with diseased area
❌ Don't include too much background
❌ Don't photograph multiple leaves
```

#### Timing
```
✅ Best: Morning or evening light
✅ Good: Cloudy day (even lighting)
❌ Avoid: Midday harsh sun
❌ Avoid: Very early/late (too dark)
```

### 🎯 Disease Detection Tips

#### What to Photograph
```
✅ Leaves with clear symptoms
✅ Recent disease signs
✅ Single affected leaf
❌ Healthy leaves
❌ Very old damage
❌ Multiple overlapping leaves
```

#### When to Use App
```
✅ First signs of disease appear
✅ Symptoms are clearly visible
✅ Need quick identification
❌ Plant is completely dead
❌ Symptoms are very minor
```
